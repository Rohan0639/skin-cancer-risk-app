Skin Lesion Risk Screening

Explainable risk assessment powered by PanDerm, a dermatology foundation model — running entirely in your browser. Your photo is never uploaded anywhere.

⚠️ This is not a medical device. It has not been clinically validated, it does not provide a diagnosis, and its output must never be used to rule out skin cancer. If you are worried about a mark on your skin — or it is changing, bleeding, itching, or not healing — see a doctor regardless of what this tool says.

About this tool

PanDerm (ViT-B/16, a dermatology foundation model) runs in your browser via ONNX Runtime Web. It classifies six lesion types and derives a risk tier from the diagnosis, alongside a classical ABCDE check computed independently of the network.

Measured on 330 held-out real clinical photographs from the ISIC Archive: macro F1 0.707, accuracy 0.721, ROC-AUC 0.946, melanoma recall 0.90. An image-only ablation scores 0.668, so the photo does most of the work and the age/site metadata adds about 0.04 — but it does shift borderline cases, so changing those fields can change the answer. The browser model is quantised to int8 to fit an 85 MB download. Datasets over-represent lighter skin, so expect worse performance on darker skin.
